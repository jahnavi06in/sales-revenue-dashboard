Enter # InsightWorks – Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard

InsightWorks is an interactive Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard built using Python, Pandas, Streamlit, and Altair.

The dashboard helps analyze sales performance, revenue trends, products, companies, and regional performance through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

## Features

- Total Sales KPI
- Total Revenue KPI
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value
- Monthly Sales & Revenue Trends
- Top Performing Products
- Company Performance Analysis
- Regional Revenue Analysis
- Category-wise Sales Analysis
- Interactive Filters
- Business Insights

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Streamlit
- Altair
- CSV Dataset

## Dataset

The dataset contains sales records for hardware companies, including:

- Date
- Company
- Product
- Category
- Region
- Quantity
- Sales
- Revenue

## Dashboard Sections

### Dashboard
Overview of important sales and revenue KPIs.

### Sales
Analyze sales trends, revenue trends, and category performance.

### Products
Identify top-performing products and product categories.

### Companies
Compare revenue and sales performance across companies.

### Regions
Analyze regional sales and revenue performance.

### Analytics
Explore deeper business insights from the sales data.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to understand sales and revenue data using interactive dashboards and generate meaningful business insights for better decision-making.
